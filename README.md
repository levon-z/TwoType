# TwoType

**Project 02 of [TwoCode](https://twocode.nl): type, and watch it grow.**

Every letter you type grows vines, leaves and roses. Pick one of 10 palettes and 7 loops, then export a PNG, an SVG or a video. Made for title cards and covers.

▶ **Try it:** [levon-z.github.io/TwoType](https://levon-z.github.io/TwoType/) or [twocode.nl/type](https://twocode.nl/type/)

![TwoType](screenshot.png)

## Files

- `index.html`: the page
- `assets/twotype.js`: the app (a built bundle)
- `assets/twocode.css`: the TwoCode website styles

Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.
